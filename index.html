Jaguar

Jaguar is a British luxury vehicle brand renowned for its performance cars, sedans, and SUVs, founded in 1933 and now a subsidiary of Tata Motors under Jaguar Land Rover. Known for combining "dramatic design" with high performance, the brand is currently pivoting toward electric vehicles and modern luxury, featuring iconic models like the E-Type in its history and the F-PACE in its current lineup.
